About LocalDoc

AM Amaad Mazari Founder & Security Architect Updated September 2026

Our Core Privacy Mandate

  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Every tool executes strictly inside your browser's private WebAssembly & RAM memory space.
  • No Data Harvesting: Your confidential contracts, tax returns, and biometric photos are never transmitted over the internet.
  • Unlimited Free Access: Because processing runs on your local CPU/GPU, there are no expensive server infrastructure bills, allowing us to keep all 29 tools completely free forever.

The Problem with Cloud PDF Services

For over a decade, internet users have been conditioned to upload sensitive personal files — legal contracts, medical records, bank statements, tax filings, and biometric identity card photos — to remote cloud servers simply to compress a file, merge two pages, or crop a passport photo.

Once uploaded to a remote cloud server, users have zero guarantee of when or if their data will be permanently deleted, whether it will be used to train proprietary AI models, or if it will be exposed in server-side data breaches. Traditional cloud converter business models rely on harvesting user files, selling subscriptions, or monetizing storage.

The LocalDoc Paradigm: Client-Side WebAssembly Architecture

LocalDoc was founded in 2021 by Amaad Mazari to challenge this outdated, insecure paradigm. By leveraging modern browser standards — including WebAssembly (Wasm), Web Workers, HTML5 Canvas, and client-side cryptography — LocalDoc compiles the full power of enterprise desktop utilities directly into the browser tab.

Editorial, Testing & Benchmarking Standards

LocalDoc maintains strict editorial and technical standards to ensure all guides, tools, and benchmarks represent verifiable engineering truths:

  • Empirical Hardware Testing: Every technical guide published in our knowledge base is grounded in physical benchmarks conducted on real machines (Apple Silicon M-series, Intel/AMD x86_64 workstations, and mobile devices) using browser profiling tools.
  • Zero Sponsored Bias: LocalDoc does not accept paid reviews, sponsored software placements, or affiliate distortions. Our technical comparisons evaluate client-side versus cloud architectures strictly based on cryptographic security, memory consumption, and execution throughput.
  • International Standards Alignment: Our documentation and tools strictly adhere to recognized global standards:
    • ISO 32000-1 / ISO 32000-2: Core document management and Portable Document Format standards.
    • W3C WebAssembly Core Specification: Secure binary code format for client-side execution.
    • ICAO Document 9303: Machine Readable Travel Documents (MRTD) specifications for biometric passport photography.
    • NIST Special Publication 800-88 Revision 1: Guidelines for Media Sanitization and digital redaction.
