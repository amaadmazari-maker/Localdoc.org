🍪 Privacy & Cookie Preferences

LocalDoc operates on a 100% Zero-Upload, client-side architecture. Your documents, photos, and calculations are processed solely in your browser RAM and are never uploaded to any server. We use essential local storage for app preferences and anonymous telemetry to improve performance. Learn more in our Privacy Policy.
