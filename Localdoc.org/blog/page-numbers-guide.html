1. Organizing Large Multi-Page Documents for Print

When merging invoices, academic dissertations, or legal briefs from different sources, original page numbering frequently becomes inconsistent or missing entirely. Adding uniform page numbers is essential for professional document presentation.

LocalDoc computes dynamic page counts and stamps clean, aligned headers and footers across large document collections in memory.

2. Automated Margin & Alignment Calculation

Our engine calculates page dimensions dynamically (handling both portrait and landscape pages within the same document) and centers the 'Page X of Y' string precisely 30 points above the bottom margin.

4. Pro Tips for Maximum Quality

  1. Choose 'Bottom Center' for standard business reports and 'Bottom Right' for formal academic submissions.
  2. Merge all your document chapters prior to stamping page numbers so total counts match.
  3. Ensure bottom margins have at least 0.5 inches of clearance to prevent overlapping text.
