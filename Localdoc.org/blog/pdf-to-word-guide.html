1. The Mathematical Challenge of PDF Text Extraction

Unlike Word documents which understand concepts like 'paragraphs', 'margins', and 'tables', a PDF file is simply a canvas of raw coordinate instructions (e.g. 'draw character A at X=120, Y=450'). Converting this back into an editable Word document requires intelligent spatial clustering.

LocalDoc's in-browser parser clusters glyphs based on vertical line heights and horizontal word spacing to accurately reconstruct natural paragraphs and structural headings.

2. Compiling Native OpenXML DOCX Archives

Once text hierarchies are extracted, LocalDoc generates a valid Microsoft OpenXML package containing document.xml, [Content_Types].xml, and relationship files. The output opens cleanly in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, and LibreOffice with zero formatting corruptions.
