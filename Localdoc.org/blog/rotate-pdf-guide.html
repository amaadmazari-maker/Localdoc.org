1. Why Viewer Rotation is Not Permanent

Rotating a document in Adobe Acrobat or Chrome viewer often only changes the temporary display view on your screen. When emailed to a client or submitted to an official portal, the PDF reverts to its original upside-down orientation.

LocalDoc permanently updates the internal /Rotate dictionary tag in the PDF catalog, ensuring it displays correctly on all devices and physical printers.

2. Zero Quality Loss in Vector Rotation

Because rotation simply adjusts the coordinate transformation matrix of the page dictionary, zero rasterization occurs. Fonts remain vector-sharp, images retain original DPI, and file size does not increase.
