SECURITY & ARCHITECTURE

How does LocalDoc ensure zero server uploads? ▾

LocalDoc is built entirely on client-side web technologies including WebAssembly, HTML5 File API, and Web Workers. When you open a document or photo, the binary data is loaded exclusively into your local browser RAM. No packets or byte streams are transmitted over the internet to remote servers, preventing document harvesting and data breach risks.

SECURITY & ARCHITECTURE

Can I use LocalDoc completely offline? ▾

Yes. LocalDoc is an air-gapped Progressive Web App (PWA). Once loaded, our Service Worker caches all scripts, WebAssembly binaries, and stylesheets locally on your machine. You can disconnect your Wi-Fi, turn on Airplane Mode, and execute all 20+ tools without any internet connection.

Because LocalDoc operates strictly on your physical device with zero server transmission, files never leave your legal custody. SCIENTIFIC CALCULATORS

How do I enter and solve complex numbers on a scientific calculator? ▾

Press MODE 2 (CMPLX) to enter complex calculation mode. Input the imaginary unit $i$ by pressing the ENG key (e.g. 12 + 5 [ENG]). To convert from rectangular to polar phasor coordinates ($r\angle\theta$), press SHIFT -> 2 (CMPLX) -> 3 (►r∠θ) -> =. This returns the magnitude and phase angle directly for AC circuit analysis.

SCIENTIFIC CALCULATORS

Why does my calculator display "Math ERROR" on matrix inversion? ▾

A Math ERROR occurs when attempting to invert a singular matrix whose determinant equals zero ($\det(A) = 0$). Invertible matrices must possess a non-zero determinant. Check your matrix coefficients using SHIFT -> 4 (MATRIX) -> 7:det( and ensure the determinant is non-zero before pressing the $x^{-1}$ key.

SCIENTIFIC CALCULATORS

How do I write and save a custom program on a Casio calculator? ▾

On programmable models like the Casio fx-5800P or fx-3650P II, press MODE -> PRGM -> 1:NEW. Assign a program title, then enter Casio-Basic code. Use the input prompt ?→A to ask for variable values, colons (:) to separate steps, and the output triangle (◢) to display computed results on the LCD screen.

PDF PROCESSING

How does PDF compression reduce file size without ruining text? ▾

LocalDoc compresses PDFs through stream defragmentation and FlateDecode Huffman compression. Vector fonts, text glyph outlines, and line art remain 100% mathematically crisp. High-resolution raster images are downsampled to optimal target resolutions (150 DPI for web, 72 DPI for email), slashing file weights by up to 85%.

PDF PROCESSING

Can I merge encrypted or password-protected PDF files? ▾

Yes. Before merging, use LocalDoc's Unlock PDF tool to decrypt the file directly in your browser using your known password. Once unlocked, the decrypted PDF streams can be freely combined, reordered, and exported in the Merge PDF tool.

PDF PROCESSING

How does LocalDoc convert scanned PDFs into editable text? ▾

LocalDoc incorporates Tesseract OCR compiled to WebAssembly. The engine runs optical character recognition directly inside a Web Worker on your device, extracting machine-encoded typography from scanned PDFs or images into selectable text, Word documents, or searchable PDFs.

BIOMETRICS & ID PHOTOS

What are the dimensions for NADRA CNIC smart card photos? ▾

Pakistani NADRA smart card ID photos must be 354x472 pixels (35x45mm) at 300 DPI with a plain light blue or white background. The subject's face must occupy 70% to 80% of the frame. Our CNIC Photo Maker automatically formats and tiles 8 identical prints onto a standard 4x6 inch photographic paper layout.

BIOMETRICS & ID PHOTOS

How do I crop photos to standard 2x2 inch US and Schengen Visa specs? ▾

Use LocalDoc's Passport Photo Maker. Select the 2x2 inch (51x51mm) preset for US Visas or 35x45mm for Schengen Visas. The interactive face alignment oval guides proper head size, eye level, and chin positioning before generating printable 4x6 inch sheets at 300 DPI.

BIOMETRICS & ID PHOTOS

How does CamScanner Studio lay out Front & Back ID cards on one A4 sheet? ▾

In CamScanner Studio, select the "ID Card Mode". Upload or snap both the front and back of your driving license, national ID, or work badge. The engine deskews both images, applies shadow removal, and positions both cards centered vertically on a single A4 page ready for official PDF submission.
