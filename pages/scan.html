How LocalDoc Rectifies & Scans Documents in Device RAM

LocalDoc Document Scanner provides an in-browser computer vision studio engineered as an open, private alternative to closed commercial scanning apps. Whether you are digitizing multi-page signed legal contracts, tax receipts, or handwritten study notes, our client-side matrix algorithms rectify lens tilt, eliminate non-uniform lighting gradients, and export crisp 300 DPI A4 PDFs — with zero bytes transmitted to remote servers.

Computer Vision Pipeline: Homography & Adaptive Filtering

When you photograph a flat document at an oblique angle, the rectangular page is projected as an irregular quadrilateral due to perspective distortion. LocalDoc applies an 8-Degree-of-Freedom (8-DOF) Projective Homography transformation:

  • Perspective Rectification (Homography): By tracking the 4 detected corner coordinates (x, y), the engine solves a system of 8 linear equations via Gaussian elimination to determine the 3x3 transformation matrix. The angled quadrilateral is backward-mapped onto an orthogonal A4 coordinate grid using bilinear pixel interpolation, eliminating perspective keystoning without pixel loss.
  • Adaptive Gaussian Binarization: Smartphone cameras often cast hand shadows and register warm indoor lighting. Rather than applying a blunt global threshold (which turns shadowed areas pitch black), our pipeline calculates a localized sliding window threshold with Gaussian weighting. Text strokes remain crisp and dark, while paper backgrounds are normalized to clean white.
  • Hardware Sensor Realities & Optical Limits: Real-world caveat: software perspective correction cannot reconstruct pixels lost to severe camera motion blur or low-light sensor noise (high ISO grain). For optimal OCR legibility and legal archiving, hold your camera parallel to the document under diffuse, direct lighting.
🎯 4-Corner Homography Crop

Precision quadrilateral warping maps arbitrary camera angles back to a true rectangular A4 or US Letter plane. Micro-adjustment handles enable sub-pixel corner alignment with loupe magnification.

✨ Adaptive Shadow Removal

Local neighborhood thresholding eliminates mobile hand shadows and desk glare without clipping high-frequency pen strokes, signatures, or official notary stamps.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Single Scan and Batch Mode?

Single Scan mode provides immediate crop-and-filter verification after every snap, with an interactive 4-corner magnifying loupe, and then automatically returns to the camera. Continuous Batch Mode allows zero-interruption high-speed shooting: each page is automatically detected, perspective-rectified, filtered with your last-used settings, and saved to IndexedDB while the live camera viewfinder remains active.

How does the stackable filter pipeline (CLAHE, De-Moiré, Sharpen) work?

LocalDoc processes scanned pages through a strict 4-stage pipeline: 1. Base Preset (Natural, Magic Color, Clean B&W, or Grayscale), followed by 2. CLAHE (Local Adaptive Histogram Equalization on an 8x8 grid to rescue faint pencil and shadowed writing), 3. De-Moiré (subpixel bilateral smoothing to remove LCD screen patterns and glossy glare), and 4. Asymmetric Unsharp Masking (crisping ink strokes without creating halos on paper borders). Filters can be stacked and customized via the advanced parameters drawer.

Can I scan large sessions of 100 to 200+ pages without crashing or losing my work?

Yes. LocalDoc uses an IndexedDB storage engine that bypasses traditional 5MB browser storage limits, automatically committing each page immediately to client-side persistent storage. Even if your phone battery dies or the browser tab closes, your entire session is preserved and instantly recovered upon reopening.

What happens if my phone battery dies or the browser tab closes during a long scanning session?

Your scanned document is 100% safe. Every captured page is stored with transaction safety in IndexedDB. When you reopen LocalDoc Document Scanner, your draft document and all previous pages are automatically loaded and ready for additional scanning, reordering, or PDF export.

Does this scanner add any watermarks to exported PDF files?

No. LocalDoc exports 100% clean, watermark-free PDFs and JPEGs suitable for legal contracts, court submissions, and university applications.

How does the perspective correction work without cloud servers?

The tool uses an 8-Degree-of-Freedom (8-DOF) Projective Homography algorithm running directly on your CPU/GPU via HTML5 Canvas and typed arrays. By tracking 4 corner pins, it solves the 3x3 transformation matrix to rectify tilted angles into a flat rectangular plane in milliseconds.

Can I scan both sides of my National Identity Card (CNIC / Driver License)?

Yes. Capture the front and back sides sequentially, and export as a single multi-page PDF or use our companion CNIC & ID Card Studio to align both sides automatically onto a single standardized A4 photocopy sheet.

What lighting conditions produce the highest quality digital scans?

For sharpest OCR text and clean binarization, place documents on a flat, contrasting surface under balanced ambient lighting. Avoid holding your smartphone directly between a single point-source ceiling lamp and the paper, which casts strong dark hand shadows.

Is my scanned document uploaded to any remote server or AI database?

Never. All image buffers, pixel transformations, and PDF compilations execute 100% inside your client device's browser memory via sandboxed WebAssembly and HTML5 Canvas. Zero bytes leave your machine.

Studio Scanning Master Guide

STEP 1 15°–20° Tilt Sensor

Frame Document & Angle Camera (15°–20°)

Place paper on a high-contrast dark tabletop. Avoid holding your phone directly parallel overhead—that casts hand shadows. Tilt camera 15°–20° towards ambient light; our 3x3 homography engine will un-tilt the page back to a flat, true 90° rectangle.

Anti-Glare Rule: Turn off camera flash on glossy flyers or laminated IDs to prevent permanent white blowout.
STEP 2 2.5x Loupe Rectification

Fine-Tune Corners with the 2.5x Loupe

The computer vision worker auto-snaps corners in <40ms. When dragging pins, a 2.5x magnifying loupe floats 75px above your finger, letting you see individual paper fibers and stamps without finger occlusion.

Margin Safety: Keep corners 2–3mm outside ink text to protect notary stamps and signatures from cropping.
STEP 3 Sauvola & Magic Color

Select Optical Enhancement Filter

Choose Magic Color for flyers and receipts, Clean B&W (Sauvola 32x32 local window) to eliminate shadows and background yellowing on contracts, or Auto-Enhanced Color to preserve photo IDs and colored stamps without bleaching.

Sauvola Magic: Calculates dynamic threshold pixel-by-pixel, rescuing faint 0.5mm pencil and signatures.

Camera & Lighting

Auto-edge detection fails or snaps to desk shadows

Probable Cause: Low contrast between document and tabletop, or dim room lighting (<20 Lux).
Immediate Fix: Place the document on a dark contrasting surface (dark desk pad, black folder, or mousepad). Ensure room light is at least 50 Lux.
Camera & Lighting

Heavy hand or phone silhouette shadows across the document

Probable Cause: Holding phone directly parallel underneath a direct overhead light fixture.
Immediate Fix: Tilt camera 15°–20° towards the light source and step back. The 3x3 homography warp will automatically restore rectangular geometry.
Camera & Lighting

Blinding white reflection spots on glossy flyer or ID card

Probable Cause: Phone camera flash reflecting off plastic surface, clipping pixel sensors to pure white (255, 255, 255).
Immediate Fix: Turn off camera flash completely. Use indirect natural daylight from a side window or diffuse ceiling bounce.
Crop & Perspective

Paragraph text, notary seals, or signatures clipped off

Probable Cause: Automated corner detection anchored slightly inside the ink border.
Immediate Fix: Drag the magnetic corner pins outward; use the 2.5x magnifying loupe floating above your finger to place pins right at the paper edge.

Faint 0.5mm pencil formulas or light blue ink disappears in B&W

Probable Cause: Global thresholding cutoff erasing faint high-frequency pencil graphite.
Immediate Fix: Switch filter to "Auto-Enhanced Color" or adjust the Sauvola sensitivity slider in Filter Studio to recover delicate mid-tones.
