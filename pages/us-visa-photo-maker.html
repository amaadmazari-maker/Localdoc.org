🇺🇸 US Department of State Official Photo Acceptance Checklist

Make sure your uploaded portrait satisfies these strict biometric rules before submitting to the DS-160 portal:

✓
Pure White Background: Plain white or off-white background with zero shadows, patterns, or furniture.
✗
No Eyeglasses: Eyeglasses are strictly prohibited since Nov 2016, even prescription glasses.
✓
Face Centering: Head must be centered, measuring 50% to 69% (1" to 1 3/8") of total image height.
✓
Neutral Expression: Both eyes open, looking directly into camera with mouth closed and natural smile.
✓
Strict File Ceiling: Digital file size must not exceed 240 KB (LocalDoc clamps to ≤200 KB).
✗
No Uniforms or Headgear: Everyday attire only. Religious headwear permitted if face oval is visible.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the exact US Visa (DS-160) photo requirements in pixels and KB?

The US Department of State requires a square 1:1 image between 600 x 600 pixels and 1200 x 1200 pixels. The file size must be strictly 240 KB or less, in JPEG (.jpg) format, with 24-bit sRGB color.

Are eyeglasses allowed in US passport and visa photos?

No. Under US Department of State regulations effective November 1, 2016, eyeglasses are no longer permitted in passport and visa photos, even if worn daily for vision correction, except in rare documented medical circumstances.

Why does the DS-160 portal reject my photo upload?

Common DS-160 photo rejection causes include file size exceeding 240 KB, non-square dimensions, shadows behind the ears or on the face, low resolution below 600x600 px, or wearing glasses.

How is the Canada PR photo different from the US Visa photo?

US Visa photos are strictly square 2 x 2 inches (51 x 51 mm / 600 x 600 px). Canadian PR and visa photos are rectangular 50 x 70 mm (2 x 2 3/4 inches) with face height between 31 mm and 36 mm.
