Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert PNG with a transparent background to PDF?

Yes. PNG transparency is handled during conversion. Transparent areas will typically render with a white background in the PDF output, which is standard for print-ready documents.

How many PNG files can I combine into one PDF?

There is no hard limit. You can combine as many PNG images as your browser can handle in memory. For very large collections (100+ images), processing may take a few additional seconds.

Is there a difference between PNG to PDF and JPG to PDF?

The main difference is image quality. PNG files are lossless and support transparency, making them better for screenshots and graphics. JPG uses lossy compression, making them smaller and better suited for photographs. Both can be converted to PDF with LocalDoc.

Can I reorder PNG images before converting?

Images are included in the PDF in the order they were added. To control page order, add your PNG files in the sequence you want them to appear in the final PDF document.

What page size is used for the PDF output?

Each PNG image is embedded on a PDF page sized to fit the image dimensions at 72 DPI. For A4-standardized output, use the image resize option or the Compress PDF tool for post-processing.

PNG to PDF — Lossless Document Creation Guide

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Does LocalDoc preserve PNG transparency when converting to PDF?
Yes. RGBA PNG files (transparent backgrounds) are embedded with an alpha mask using PDF's image mask operator. The transparent areas appear white in standard PDF viewers, but the original alpha data is preserved in the PDF stream.
What happens with 16-bit PNG files?
16-bit PNG (common in photography RAW workflows) is down-sampled to 8-bit per channel before PDF embedding, as PDF 1.7 supports a maximum of 8 bits per component in JPEG streams.
Can I batch-convert a folder of PNG screenshots?
Yes. Drag a folder or multi-select up to 500 PNG files. The tool arranges them in filename order by default, which you can then reorder by drag-and-drop before generating the PDF.
What compression is applied to PNG images in the PDF?
PNG streams are re-encoded using JPEG compression inside the PDF (at the quality setting you select). For truly lossless embedding, use the "No Compression" option which embeds the PNG as a raw Flate-compressed stream.
