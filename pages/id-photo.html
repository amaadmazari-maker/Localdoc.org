Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exact size of a Pakistan CNIC card in inches and mm?

A Pakistan CNIC measures exactly 3.370 × 2.125 inches (85.60 × 53.98 mm, or 8.56 × 5.40 cm) with a 3.18 mm rounded corner radius. At 300 DPI print quality, this corresponds to 1011 × 638 pixels.

What are the official NADRA CNIC photo requirements?

NADRA Pak-Identity requires photos to be exactly 354 × 472 pixels (35 × 45 mm) at 350 DPI, portrait orientation, strictly under 300 KB in JPEG format, with a plain light blue (#E0F2FE) or pure white background, looking directly at the camera with a neutral expression.

How do I print the 2-in-1 A4 ID Card Photocopy at 100% exact scale?

Download the high-res 300 DPI PDF or JPG from LocalDoc. In your printer settings, select standard A4 paper and ensure the scale setting is set to 100% (Actual Size / Do Not Scale). Both sides will print at exact original dimensions with space for stamp and signature attestation.
