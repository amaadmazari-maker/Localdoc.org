Why Use 2-in-1 ID Card Photocopying?

Government agencies, banks, consulates, and legal institutions around the world require double-sided identity verification documents where both the front and back of your ID card or CNIC are presented together on a single side of an A4 page.

Frequently Asked Questions About 2-in-1 ID Photocopies

How do I capture a clean photo of my ID card?

Place your card flat on a plain dark or contrasting surface under good lighting without flash. Hold your phone directly above the card so the edges are straight.

What document types are supported?

Any standard credit-card-sized identity document: Pakistan CNIC / NICOP / Smart Card, US State Driver's Licenses, National Identity Cards, Student IDs, and Health Insurance Cards.

Can I download in PDF format for email submissions?

Yes! Click the Download A4 PDF button to generate an official single-page PDF document ready for attaching to job, bank, or visa portal submissions.
