Frequently Asked Questions

What are the official NADRA CNIC photo requirements?

NADRA Pak-Identity requires photos to be exactly 354 × 472 pixels, portrait orientation, under 300 KB, with a plain light blue or white background, looking directly at the camera with a neutral expression.

How do I print the 2-in-1 A4 ID Card Photocopy?

Download the A4 PDF or high-res JPG, open on any device, and print directly onto standard A4 paper at 100% scale (no scaling / fit to page disabled).
