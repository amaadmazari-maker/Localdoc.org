Standard Official Photo Dimensions & Target File Sizes

International Passport (35 × 45 mm)

Standard standard for UK, EU Schengen, Canada, Australia, and Asian passports. Corresponds to 413 × 531 pixels at 300 DPI, typically under 50KB or 100KB for online visa portals.

US Visa & Green Card (2 × 2 inches / 51 × 51 mm)

Required by the US Department of State (DS-160 / Diversity Visa Lottery). Exact 600 × 600 pixels to 1200 × 1200 pixels at 300 DPI, strictly under 240KB.

Government & Exam Portals (Under 20KB or 50KB)

Civil service examinations, university admission portals, and national ID cards frequently require uploads strictly between 10KB and 50KB. Our binary search compression hits your exact target limit every time.
