Frequently Asked Questions About Passport Photos

Can I wear eyeglasses or sunglasses in my passport photo?

No. Eyeglasses of any kind (prescription glasses, reading glasses, or tinted lenses) are strictly prohibited under US Department of State and international ICAO regulations to prevent flash glare and obscured eye landmarks.

What facial expression should I have?

Maintain a neutral facial expression with both eyes open and looking directly into the camera lens, mouth closed, and no teeth showing.

How do I print the 4x6 inch sheet at a local pharmacy or print shop?

Download the 4x6" Sheet (.jpg) and order a standard 4x6 inch photographic print at any local pharmacy (Walgreens, CVS, Boots) or standard photo printer for pennies. Then cut along the fine scissors guidelines.
