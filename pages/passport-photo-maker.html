Technical Architecture: ICAO Doc 9303 Biometric Compliance in RAM

International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) standard Doc 9303 specifies precise mathematical criteria for Machine Readable Travel Documents (MRTDs). Facial recognition gates and automated passport readers require strict geometrical symmetry:

  • Facial Height Ratio (Chin to Crown): ICAO guidelines dictate that the applicant's head from the base of the chin to the top of the hair must occupy between 70% and 80% (32 mm to 36 mm on a 45 mm photo). LocalDoc automatically scales the canvas viewport to satisfy this strict biometric constraint.
  • Eye Axis Horizontal Alignment: Automated border gates calculate inter-pupillary distance. LocalDoc aligns the horizontal eye axis at approximately 56% to 62% of the vertical frame height.
  • 300 DPI Continuous Tone Rasterization: Canvas elements are initialized with physical print formulas: Width_px = (35 / 25.4) × 300 ≈ 413 px, guaranteeing photographic lab clarity without pixel staircasing.
  • Multi-Photo 4×6 Print Sheet Tiling: When printable sheet mode is selected, an off-screen 1800 × 1200 pixel canvas (exact 4×6 inch at 300 DPI) tiles 6 photos with centered spacing and light hairline cutting marks for effortless trimming with scissors or a photo cutter.

Frequently Asked Questions About Passport Photos

Can I wear eyeglasses or sunglasses in my passport photo?

No. Eyeglasses of any kind (prescription glasses, reading glasses, or tinted lenses) are strictly prohibited under US Department of State and international ICAO regulations. Glare on lenses or frame shadows obstruct automated iris scanning systems.

What facial expression should I have?

Maintain a neutral facial expression with both eyes open, looking directly into the camera lens, and your mouth naturally closed. Smiling with teeth showing or grimacing can cause automated biometric rejection.

What should I wear for a passport photo?

Wear normal everyday street clothing that contrasts with the white background (e.g. dark navy, black, or rich colors). Avoid white shirts that blend into the background, uniforms, camouflage patterns, or hats/head coverings (unless for documented religious purposes).

How should I print the 4x6 inch photo sheet?

Download the 4x6" printable sheet and order standard 4x6 inch photographic prints at your local pharmacy, supermarket, or photo kiosk (costing around $0.35). Choose glossy or matte photo paper. Ensure the print service does not crop or scale the image.

Can I take a passport photo with my smartphone camera?

Yes. Modern smartphone cameras capture exceptionally high quality (12 MP to 48 MP). Use the rear primary camera, have a friend or family member hold the phone at eye level 1.5 meters away in a well-lit room, and upload the photo to LocalDoc.

Shadow & Lighting Troubleshooting

Harsh Wall Shadows: If you see a dark silhouette behind your head, step 1 to 2 feet forward away from the wall and ensure room lighting is diffused rather than coming from a single direct bulb overhead.

Uneven Facial Lighting: Avoid standing next to a single side window that casts half of your face in shadow. Face the light source directly so both cheeks and both ears receive balanced, natural illumination.
