Frequently Asked Questions About PAN Card Resizing

How do I resize my photo to 213x213 pixels and under 30 KB?

Select the PAN Photo (213×213 px, ≤ 30 KB) tab above. Drop your passport photo. LocalDoc automatically resamples the image to 213×213 pixels and runs a logarithmic binary search to find the highest-quality JPEG compression that stays strictly under 30 KB.

How do I resize my signature to under 12 KB?

Click the PAN Signature tab. Upload a smartphone photo of your signature signed on white paper. The tool sets the aspect ratio to 400×200 pixels and clamps the file strictly under 12 KB so it is accepted on the first attempt.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the exact photo specifications for PAN card applications?

For NSDL and UTIITSL portals, photos must be 2.5 cm x 3.5 cm (or 213 x 213 pixels at 300 DPI), in JPEG/JPG format, with a file size strictly between 20KB and 50KB.

What are the signature requirements for NSDL PAN portals?

Signatures must be signed with black ink on white paper, sized 2 cm x 4.5 cm (or 400 x 200 pixels at 200 DPI), and file size must be strictly between 10KB and 20KB.

Why do government portals reject resized photos?

Most rejections occur because the file size exceeds 50KB by even a single byte, or DPI metadata is missing. LocalDoc injects exact DPI headers and guarantees byte-accurate limits.

Can I take a photo with my smartphone?

Yes. You can take a selfie against a light background or capture your signature in good lighting. Our tool handles aspect ratio adjustment, resolution correction, and file compression.

Are my biometric photos and signatures stored online?

Never. LocalDoc runs 100% locally in your device's browser memory. Your biometric image data is never uploaded, recorded, or transmitted to any remote servers.
