Why Comparing Legal Contracts & PDFs Offline Matters

When reviewing contract amendments, NDAs, lease agreements, or vendor revisions, even a single altered digit or sentence can expose your company or personal finances to significant liability. Sneaky contract modifications often slip through standard redlines—such as changing a payment deadline from 30 days to 10 days, increasing late payment interest from 1% to 5%, or stripping away mutual indemnification clauses.

Most online PDF comparison services force you to upload confidential legal documents to third-party servers, where they may be logged, cached, or exposed to data breaches. LocalDoc processes both files 100% client-side inside your browser memory using Mozilla's pdf.js and local WebAssembly text extraction routines. No document content is ever sent over the network.

The 4 Hidden Contract Revisions to Watch For

💰 1. Monetary Figures & Payment Schedules

Watch for retainer fee increases, subtle percentage changes in royalties or penalties, and shifted payment terms (e.g., from Net 30 to Net 15).

📅 2. Notice Windows & Termination Triggers

Parties often attempt to change termination "for convenience" to "immediate termination for breach" or drastically shorten notice periods.

⚖️ 3. Unilateral Indemnification Shifts

A mutual indemnification clause where both sides protect each other may be covertly converted into a one-sided indemnity where only you absorb legal risks.

📄 4. Added or Missing Appendices

Our page discrepancy detector immediately alerts you if Document B contains extra pages (like an added fee schedule) or missing exhibit pages.
