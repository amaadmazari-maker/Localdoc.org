Frequently Asked Questions About Signing PDFs Online

Is an electronic signature legally binding on commercial contracts?

Yes. In the United States (ESIGN Act / UETA), the UK, Canada, Australia, and the European Union (eIDAS), standard electronic signatures carry equal legal standing to wet-ink pen signatures for most commercial contracts, employment offers, non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), and leases.

Can I sign with my finger on a smartphone or tablet?

Yes. The signature pad is designed with touch event listeners and hardware-accelerated BÃ©zier smoothing, providing an exceptionally natural, pen-like feel when signing on iPhones, iPads, and Android devices.

Can I sign multiple pages in the same document?

Under the "Target Page to Sign" dropdown, you can select "Last Page" (the standard signature block location), "First Page", or "All Pages" to automatically place initial stamps across every sheet of your contract.

Will signing a PDF degrade the original text quality?

No. LocalDoc injects the signature image as an isolated overlay stream on top of the existing vector page coordinates. Text, fonts, tables, and existing artwork remain completely lossless.

Documents Requiring Notarization

Exceptions to Electronic Signatures: Certain formal legal documents — including wills, codicils, testamentary trusts, family law divorce decrees, and specific real estate deed transfers — may require physical in-person wet ink signatures or certified notary seals by local statutory law. Consult legal counsel for specialized jurisdictional filings.
