Technical Architecture: ESIGN Act & In-Memory Signature Injection

Under the United States Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (ESIGN, 15 U.S.C. § 7001) and the European Union eIDAS Regulation (No 910/2014), an electronic signature carries full legal enforceability when intent to sign, association with the record, and record integrity are maintained:

  • Quadratic Bézier Curve Smoothing: The HTML5 signature canvas captures continuous pointer movements (pointerdown, pointermove, pointerup) and interpolates midpoint tangents using quadratic Bézier mathematics, eliminating robotic jagged edges.
  • Alpha Channel PNG Form XObject Embedding: The drawn signature is exported as a transparent 32-bit RGBA PNG buffer in RAM and embedded into the target PDF's resource dictionary as an independent Form XObject.
  • Certified Execution Timestamping: A standardized ISO timestamp string is stamped into the appearance stream using standard Helvetica font coordinates, establishing verifiable execution chronology.
  • Absolute Confidentiality Guarantee: Traditional electronic signature platforms (DocuSign, Adobe Sign, HelloSign) store every contract, financial clause, and personal signature on remote cloud databases. LocalDoc runs 100% inside your browser's memory, ensuring zero third-party disclosure.

Frequently Asked Questions About Signing PDFs Online

Is an electronic signature legally binding on commercial contracts?

Yes. In the United States (ESIGN Act / UETA), the UK, Canada, Australia, and the European Union (eIDAS), standard electronic signatures carry equal legal standing to wet-ink pen signatures for most commercial contracts, employment offers, non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), and leases.

Can I sign with my finger on a smartphone or tablet?

Yes. The signature pad is designed with touch event listeners and hardware-accelerated Bézier smoothing, providing an exceptionally natural, pen-like feel when signing on iPhones, iPads, and Android devices.

Can I sign multiple pages in the same document?

Under the "Target Page to Sign" dropdown, you can select "Last Page" (the standard signature block location), "First Page", or "All Pages" to automatically place initial stamps across every sheet of your contract.

Will signing a PDF degrade the original text quality?

No. LocalDoc injects the signature image as an isolated overlay stream on top of the existing vector page coordinates. Text, fonts, tables, and existing artwork remain completely lossless.

Documents Requiring Notarization

Exceptions to Electronic Signatures: Certain formal legal documents — including wills, codicils, testamentary trusts, family law divorce decrees, and specific real estate deed transfers — may require physical in-person wet ink signatures or certified notary seals by local statutory law. Consult legal counsel for specialized jurisdictional filings.
