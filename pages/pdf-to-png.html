What is the difference between PDF to PNG and PDF to JPG?

PNG uses lossless compression and supports transparent backgrounds, making it superior for text, charts, diagrams, and logos. JPG is lossy and best suited for photographs.

PDF to PNG — Lossless Extraction Technical Guide

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Why choose PNG over JPG for PDF page extraction?
PNG uses lossless compression — ideal for PDFs containing text, diagrams, line art, or screenshots where compression artefacts would be visible. JPG is lossy and introduces ringing at text edges. Use PNG for archiving; JPG for web display.
Does PDF to PNG support transparency?
Yes. Pages with transparent backgrounds (common in branded PDFs with no background fill) are exported as RGBA PNG-32, preserving the alpha channel.
What is the typical file size increase from PDF to PNG?
A 1 MB single-page PDF typically exports to a 300 DPI PNG of 2–8 MB depending on content complexity. Vector content at high DPI produces large PNGs; simplify to 150 DPI for smaller outputs.
Is there a page limit for batch PNG export?
LocalDoc processes up to 500 pages in a single session. For documents over 100 pages, processing is batched in groups of 25 to prevent browser memory exhaustion.
