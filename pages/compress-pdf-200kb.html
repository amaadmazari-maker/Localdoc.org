Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress a PDF to under 200 KB without making text blurry?

In LocalDoc, PDF vector typography is preserved losslessly. Text is not converted into a bitmap, meaning it stays mathematically sharp at 500% zoom. Only embedded high-resolution photos are optimized to fit the 200 KB threshold.

Why do government and exam portals reject PDFs over 200 KB?

Government web portals handle hundreds of thousands of simultaneous applicants. Enforcing a strict 200 KB or 100 KB limit prevents server crashes, reduces infrastructure storage costs, and guarantees fast form upload speeds.

What if my PDF is still larger than 200 KB after compression?

If your document contains numerous full-page color scans, click the Under 100 KB (Extreme) preset. Alternatively, use our Split PDF tool to remove unnecessary pages before compressing.

Can I compress multiple PDFs to 200 KB in a batch?

Yes. You can drag and drop multiple files at once. LocalDoc will compress all documents in local RAM and provide a single convenient ZIP download containing all 200 KB files.
