Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert iPhone HEIC photos directly to PDF?

Yes. LocalDoc includes built-in offline WebAssembly decoding for Apple iPhone HEIC and HEIF photos. You do not need to convert your camera roll images to JPG first.

What is the difference between "Fit to Standard A4" and "Original Photo Dimensions"?

"Fit to Standard A4" resizes each photo to fit within an international standard sheet of paper with safety margins, ideal for office printing and official document filings. "Original Photo Dimensions" sets each PDF page to match the exact pixel aspect ratio and resolution of each photo (such as 1:1 square photos or long receipts) without white padding.

How does Smart Auto-Sorting fix mobile photo ordering?

Standard computer sorting puts file "photo_10.jpg" before "photo_2.jpg". LocalDoc uses natural alphanumeric collation so numbers sort in true numerical sequence (1, 2, 3... 10). It also reads EXIF camera metadata to reconstruct the exact real-world order your photos were snapped.

Can I share the generated PDF directly on WhatsApp or mobile apps?

Yes! On mobile devices supporting native file sharing, the "Share PDF" button launches your system share sheet so you can send the document straight to WhatsApp chats, Gmail, or Slack without saving it to disk first.

Are my private photos uploaded to remote servers?

Never. LocalDoc operates with an uncompromising zero-upload architecture. All decoding, EXIF inspection, canvas scaling, and PDF embedding happen 100% inside your local device RAM.

JPG to PDF — Batch Conversion Technical Guide

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

How many JPG images can I convert to a single PDF?
Up to 500 images in a single session. Images are processed in batches of 50 to manage memory. Each image is embedded as a compressed JPEG stream within the PDF — no re-encoding quality loss occurs.
Can I set a custom page size for each image?
Yes. Choose A4, Letter, A3, Legal, or "Fit to Image" (page sized exactly to the pixel dimensions of each photo). Mixed page sizes within a single PDF are supported.
What image formats are accepted besides JPG?
PNG, WebP, GIF (first frame), BMP, and AVIF are also accepted. All formats are decoded via the browser's native Image decoder and re-embedded as JPEG or PNG streams in the PDF.
How is image quality preserved during conversion?
LocalDoc embeds images as raw JPEG streams inside the PDF without re-encoding. This means your original JPEG quality (e.g. 95%) is preserved exactly — no additional generation loss.
