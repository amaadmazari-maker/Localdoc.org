Frequently Asked Technical Questions

What DPI settings are available for PDF to JPG export?
LocalDoc offers 72 DPI (web-optimised), 150 DPI (standard quality), 300 DPI (print quality), and custom DPI up to 600. At 300 DPI, an A4 page renders to 2480×3508 pixels — suitable for professional printing.
How are colour spaces handled during conversion?
PDF pages may contain CMYK, RGB, or greyscale colour spaces. LocalDoc's WebAssembly renderer converts CMYK to sRGB automatically using an ICC profile-aware colour transform.
Are PDF fonts rendered accurately in the JPG?
Type 1, TrueType, and OpenType fonts are rendered using the pdf.js font engine with sub-pixel antialiasing. CID fonts (common in CJK documents) require the embedded font to be present in the PDF for accurate rendering.
Can I extract specific page ranges?
Yes. Enter a range such as "1,3,5-9,12" to extract only those pages as separate JPG files. All pages are packaged into a single ZIP download.
