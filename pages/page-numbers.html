How to Add Page Numbers to PDF — Step by Step

  1. Upload your PDF — Drag your document into the numbering dropzone or click "Select PDF Document".
  2. Select numbering position — Choose where you want numbers to appear across headers or footers.
  3. Choose numbering template — Select whether to display simple page indices or complete "Page X of Y" pagination.
  4. Set page range & starting offset — Configure whether to skip the front cover page or begin numbering from a specific index.
  5. Download your numbered PDF — Click "Apply Page Numbers" to save your freshly paginated PDF instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I skip numbering on the cover page?

Yes. You can specify page ranges to exclude (such as Page 1 for cover sheets) so numbering begins cleanly on the introduction or table of contents page.

Will adding page numbers overwrite existing text or graphics?

Page numbers are injected into the PDF content stream as a clean overlay layer. You can adjust margins to ensure numbers sit safely in whitespace margins.

Does page numbering change the PDF format or version?

No. LocalDoc preserves the original PDF specifications, internal hyperlinks, bookmarks, and metadata dictionaries without re-rasterizing document pages.

Can I use Roman numerals or custom text prefixes?

Yes. You can select custom prefix formats such as "Section - 1" or format indices for formal academic thesis submissions and regulatory legal bundles.

Is this tool free for large documents?

Yes. LocalDoc is 100% free with no limits on document page counts. Numbering runs in your browser memory without server fees or subscription gates.

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

What numbering formats are supported?
Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3…), Roman numerals (i, ii, iii… or I, II, III…), alphabetic (a, b, c… or A, B, C…), and custom prefix formats ("Page 1 of N", "Draft-1", "Ex. A-1"). Starting number is configurable.
Where can page numbers be positioned?
9 position options: top-left, top-centre, top-right, middle-left, middle-right, bottom-left, bottom-centre, bottom-right. Margin offset is adjustable from 5 mm to 50 mm from edge.
Can I skip page numbers on certain pages?
Yes. Define skip rules: skip first N pages (for cover sheets), skip blank pages (automatically detected by content area analysis), or skip a custom page list.
What fonts are available for page numbers?
All 14 standard PDF fonts (Helvetica, Times, Courier, Symbol, ZapfDingbats) plus custom TTF/OTF upload. Font sizes from 6pt to 36pt. Color picker supports any RGB value.
