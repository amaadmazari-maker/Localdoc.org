Why Privacy-First Document Processing Matters

Most online document tools operate by uploading your files to a remote cloud server, where they are processed and then (hopefully) deleted. This model introduces serious privacy risks, especially for sensitive documents such as passports, national ID cards, contracts, medical reports, payslips, and financial statements.

LocalDoc was built around a fundamentally different model: every operation — PDF compression, format conversion, OCR, document scanning, and biometric photo cropping — runs entirely inside your own browser using WebAssembly and modern JavaScript APIs. Your files never leave your device. There are no cloud servers involved in processing, no accounts required, and no file retention policies to worry about.

This approach is particularly valuable for professionals handling confidential client documents — lawyers, accountants, doctors, surveyors, HR managers, and government officials — and for individuals in regions with strict data sovereignty laws or limited internet bandwidth. Because all processing runs locally on your CPU, LocalDoc also works fully offline after the first page load, making it reliable even in areas with unreliable internet connectivity.
