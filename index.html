Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How can LocalDoc process PDF, Word, and Excel files without uploading them?

LocalDoc loads compiled JavaScript and WebAssembly binary modules (such as PDF-Lib, Mammoth.js, SheetJS, and Tesseract.js) into your browser session once. When you select a document, your device's local CPU and RAM parse the binary streams directly in memory. No network payload is ever dispatched to our servers.

Are there file size limits or hidden subscription fees?

No. Because processing is powered by your local hardware rather than costly cloud servers, LocalDoc imposes no daily file conversion limits, no artificial waiting queues, and no premium paywalls. You can process large 100MB+ PDF documents or hundreds of photos completely free.

How does the CamScanner tool achieve 300 DPI clarity on mobile devices?

Our scanner captures the camera's raw optical sensor resolution and uses a 16x16 triangular affine matrix homography mesh to calculate projective perspective coordinates. It flattens tilted and curved pages onto a standard 300 DPI A4 canvas ($2480 \times 3508\text{ pixels}$) while applying adaptive 2D integral shadow removal.

Is LocalDoc safe for confidential legal, medical, and banking records?

Yes. LocalDoc is engineered specifically for legal teams, accounting firms, and healthcare providers requiring strict GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC-2 compliance. You can verify this independently by opening your browser's Developer Tools Network tab—you will observe exactly zero file data packets during all conversions.
