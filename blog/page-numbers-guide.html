1. The Pagination Dilemma in Assembled Documents

When legal teams assemble a litigation filing, corporate analysts compile an annual report, or students bind a Master's thesis, they often merge multiple source files—Word exports, Excel spreadsheets, scanned exhibits, and presentation slides. The resulting consolidated PDF almost always suffers from chaotic, disjointed pagination:

  • Chapter 1 ends on Page 14, but Chapter 2 starts numbering again at Page 1.
  • Financial exhibits have numbers on the top right, while legal briefs have numbers on the bottom center.
  • Cover pages and executive table of contents are inappropriately labeled as "Page 1".

LocalDoc's client-side pagination engine re-indexes the entire document collection, stamping clean, uniform, and legally compliant page numbering across every sheet directly in browser memory.

2. Dynamic Geometry: Handling Mixed Orientations & MediaBoxes

A major flaw in basic pagination tools is assuming all pages in a PDF have identical dimensions. If a document mixes Portrait A4 (595.28 x 841.89 pt) and Landscape A4 (841.89 x 595.28 pt), a naive static offset will cause numbers to float in the middle of landscape pages or be clipped off the bottom margin.

LocalDoc executes a per-page coordinate calculation:

  1. MediaBox & CropBox Inspection: Reads the exact physical width ($W$) and height ($H$) of each specific page dictionary.
  2. Margin Clearance Offset: Positions the baseline precisely Y = 28 pt (approx. 10mm) above the physical bottom edge, perfectly aligned inside the printable zone of all commercial laser copiers.
  3. Horizontal Text Centering: Computes the exact bounding width of the rendered string (e.g., "Page 14 of 52") using font metrics, ensuring the number is mathematically centered along the horizontal axis ($X = (W - \text{StringWidth}) / 2$).

4. Pro Tips for Flawless Academic & Legal Pagination

  1. Exclude Cover Pages: Check the "Skip First Page" option to keep your glossy cover clean and uncluttered, initiating "Page 1" on your document's introductory chapter.
  2. Standard Bates Stamping: For legal discovery productions, use our custom prefix format to stamp identifiers like ACME-0001, ACME-0002 across all evidentiary exhibits.
  3. Font Hierarchy: Use standard sans-serif typography (Helvetica or Arial) at 9–10 pt size in neutral dark gray (#334155) for a clean, non-intrusive appearance.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will adding page numbers overwrite existing text on my pages?

Our engine places page numbers in the standard 0.5-inch (30-point) footer margin. As long as your document has standard margins, the numbers will sit cleanly in the gutter below your body text.

Can I format numbers as "Page X of Y"?

Yes. LocalDoc supports multiple formatting templates: simple integers (1), fractional notations (1 / 50), and formal strings (Page 1 of 50).

Are my files uploaded anywhere during numbering?

Never. All PDF content streams and font injections execute 100% inside your browser's private memory sandbox. Zero bytes are transmitted to any remote server.

Does this tool work on mobile devices?

Yes. You can paginate multi-page PDF documents directly on iPhones, iPads, and Android smartphones using any modern mobile browser.
