1.1 CNIC Size in Inches (Imperial Dimensions for Word & Photoshop)

When setting up templates in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or Adobe Photoshop with Imperial rulers, the exact dimensions of a Pakistan CNIC are 3.370 inches in width and 2.125 inches in height. In fractional carpentry/printing notation, this is approximately 3⅜ inches wide by 2⅛ inches tall. The card corner rounding is exactly ⅛ inch (0.125 in / 3.18 mm) radius.

1.2 CNIC Size for Print (300 DPI vs 600 DPI Resolution)

To print a crisp, readable photocopy or PVC replacement card that will not be rejected by Pakistani banks, embassies, or government ministries, the image must be rendered at 300 DPI (Dots Per Inch) or higher:

  • Standard Print (300 DPI): Exactly 1011 × 638 pixels. This is the optimal balance for laser printers, inkjet photocopy shops, and document PDF submissions.
  • High-Definition Print (600 DPI): Exactly 2022 × 1276 pixels. Recommended for ultra-fine micro-text, QR code scanning, and biometric fingerprint ridge clarity.
  • Important Printer Setting: In your operating system print dialog, always set Page Scaling to "100%" or "Actual Size". Selecting "Fit to Printable Area" will shrink your CNIC by 3%–6%, causing bank scanners and biometric kiosks to reject it.

1.3 NIC Card vs Smart CNIC (SNIC) Dimensions: Is There Any Difference?

Many citizens wonder if the older paper-laminated National Identity Card (NIC) differs in size from the newer chip-based Smart National Identity Card (SNIC). There is no difference in dimensions. Both cards measure exactly 85.60 × 53.98 mm (3.370 × 2.125 inches). The only differences are material (polycarbonate with an embedded gold chip in the SNIC) and data encoding (MRZ machine-readable lines on the reverse side).

4. Preventing Identity Theft: Protective Purpose Watermarks: Protective Purpose Watermarks

⚠ Critical Identity Security Warning

Never submit a clean, un-watermarked photocopy of your CNIC or National ID to private vendors, retail shops, or telecom franchise operators. Unscrupulous operators can photocopy your card again and register fraudulent SIM cards, obtain illicit loans, or open shadow accounts under your name.

To protect yourself, write or stamp a semi-transparent diagonal text across the face of the copy clearly defining its sole legal purpose:

  • For Bank Accounts: FOR HBL / MEEZAN / UBL ACCOUNT OPENING ONLY - [DATE]
  • For SIM Cards: FOR JAZZ / ZONG SIM VERIFICATION ONLY - [DATE]
  • For Visa & Immigration: FOR SCHENGEN / UK VISA SUBMISSION ONLY
  • For Job Applications: FOR EMPLOYMENT VERIFICATION AT [COMPANY NAME] ONLY

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the official Pakistan CNIC size in inches?

The official size of a Pakistan NADRA CNIC in inches is exactly 3.370 × 2.125 inches (approx 3⅜ by 2⅛ inches). It features rounded corners with a ⅛-inch (0.125 in / 3.18 mm) radius, adhering strictly to the international ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 standard.

Q: What is the CNIC card size in mm and cm?

In metric measurements, a Pakistan CNIC is exactly 85.60 mm wide by 53.98 mm high (or 8.56 × 5.40 cm) with a standard card thickness of 0.76 mm.

Q: What is the exact CNIC size for print in pixels at 300 DPI?

At 300 DPI (Dots Per Inch, the international commercial print benchmark), a CNIC card is exactly 1011 pixels wide by 638 pixels high. For 600 DPI ultra-high definition printing, it is 2022 × 1276 pixels.

Q: Is the older laminated NIC card size different from the new Smart CNIC?

No. Both older laminated NICs and new Smart CNICs (SNICs) have identical physical dimensions (85.60 × 53.98 mm / 3.370 × 2.125 inches). Modern SNICs add an embedded microchip, national crest hologram, and MRZ lines on the back.

Q: Can I use this for non-Pakistani ID cards like Indian PAN or UAE Emirates ID?

Yes! All modern national identity cards, including Indian PAN cards, Aadhaar PVC cards, UAE Emirates IDs, Saudi Iqamas, and European/US driving licences, adhere to the same ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 standard (85.60 × 53.98 mm). The 2-in-1 photocopy mode works flawlessly for all of them.

Q: How do I ensure my printer prints the copy at exact 100% life-size?

When printing the downloaded PDF or JPG from your computer or phone, always verify in the print dialogue that Scale is set to "100%" or "Actual Size". Do NOT choose "Fit to Page" or "Shrink to Fit", as this will reduce the card size by 3% to 5%.

Q: What printer paper should I use for official submissions?

For bank accounts, court filings, and embassy dossiers, use standard 80 GSM A4 bond paper. If you are printing photo identity cards for lamination or badges, use 220–260 GSM glossy photo paper.
