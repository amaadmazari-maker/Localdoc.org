NADRA CNIC, NICOP & Smart Card Photo Guidelines (354x472 px)

AM Amaad Mazari Lead Document Architect Updated September 2026 10 min read

Key Takeaways & Summary

  • NADRA Dimensional Standards: Pakistan's National Database and Registration Authority mandates a precise 354 x 472 pixel (35 x 45 mm) ratio for all Smart National Identity Cards (SNIC) and overseas NICOPs.
  • Face-to-Frame Coverage: The face (from chin to hairline) must occupy between 70% and 80% of the vertical frame height.
  • Background Colors: While older laminated ID cards used light blue backgrounds, current digital registration centers and the Pak-ID mobile app recommend a uniform, shadow-free pure white or light blue backdrop.
  • 2-in-1 Photocopy Compilation: Official government, banking, and real estate transactions require both Front and Back sides of your CNIC photocopied onto a single A4 page. LocalDoc compiles this automatically in browser RAM.

1. Understanding the NADRA Pak-ID Biometric Standards

NADRA has transitioned millions of citizens to microchip-enabled Smart National Identity Cards (SNIC) and the Pak-ID mobile app. When uploading a photograph online or submitting documents at an Executive Registration Centre (NRC), automated biometric quality assessment checks verify that your facial features can be extracted for automated facial recognition.

If your submitted photo has dark shadows, incorrect aspect ratios, or reflections from glasses, the automated validation pipeline will immediately trigger an error stating: "Photograph does not meet biometric standards."

3. 2-in-1 CNIC Photocopy Sheet (Front & Back on 1-Page A4)

When opening a bank account in Pakistan, applying for a driving license, or filing property paperwork, institutions require a copy of both the front and back of your CNIC. Traditionally, this required a visit to a photocopy shop where sensitive identity documents were handled on shared public machines.
