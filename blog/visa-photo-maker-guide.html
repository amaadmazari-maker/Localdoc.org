1. The ICAO Doc 9303 Global Biometric Visa Standard

Whether you are applying for a US B1/B2 tourist visa through the CEAC DS-160 portal, a Schengen visa for European travel, or a Canadian study permit via the IRCC portal, your biometric photograph will be scrutinized by automated computer vision systems. Over 35% of visa application delays worldwide are caused by non-compliant passport photographs.

The International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) Doc 9303 outlines strict geometric and photographic parameters:

  • Head Height Ratio: The distance between the bottom of the chin and the crown of the head (top of hair) must measure between 70% and 80% of the total frame height.
  • Eye Axis Horizontal Alignment: The horizontal line passing through the centers of both eyes must sit between 56% and 69% of the height from the bottom margin.
  • Neutral Facial Expression: Both eyes open and clearly visible, mouth closed without smiling or teeth showing, and direct frontal camera gaze.

  1. Wardrobe Contrast: Wear dark, collared clothing (dark blue, black, or charcoal). Wearing a white shirt against a white wall will cause automated edge-detection bots to reject your photo.
  2. No Eyeglasses or Glare: In accordance with US State Department and global ICAO standards, eyeglasses are strictly prohibited in biometric visa photos.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I use a smartphone selfie for my visa photo?

Selfies taken at arm's length cause wide-angle facial distortion (large nose, recessed ears). Have a family member hold the camera 4 to 6 feet away at eye level, or use a timer with your phone resting on a stable surface.

Will the US DS-160 portal accept the exported file size?

Yes. When you choose the US Visa preset, LocalDoc automatically exports a 600x600 pixel square JPEG compressed to approximately 120–180 KB, comfortably below the official 240 KB ceiling.

Are religious head coverings allowed?

Yes, provided the head covering does not obscure any part of your face from the bottom of your chin to the top of your forehead. Both edges of the face must be fully visible.

Are my personal portrait photos uploaded to your servers?

Never. LocalDoc processes 100% of facial cropping, background replacement, and image scaling directly inside your browser's memory using HTML5 Canvas. No photos are ever sent over the internet.
