1. Why You Don't Need Heavy Mobile Scanning Apps

Mobile scanning apps like CamScanner often demand invasive phone permissions, display aggressive subscriptions, and place ugly watermarks across your personal contracts. With modern WebRTC browser APIs, your mobile browser has direct, hardware-accelerated access to your camera sensor.

LocalDoc turns any smartphone or laptop into an enterprise-grade document scanner with zero installations and zero cloud tracking.

Live Optical Pipeline: Real-time 8-point boundary crop, tap-to-focus, and Magic Color whitening filter.

2. Sauvola Adaptive Thresholding for Clean B&W Pages

Capturing a paper document in uneven indoor lighting usually produces dark shadows and yellow tints. LocalDoc applies a real-time Sauvola adaptive binarization algorithm that computes local mean and standard deviation across image tiles. Shadows are eliminated, background paper becomes pure white, and text inks are boosted to crisp black.
