1. Why Client-Side Word Conversion Matters for Privacy

Legal briefs, HR compensation summaries, and confidential NDAs are commonly drafted in Microsoft Word (.docx). Uploading these unredacted drafts to public online file converter websites poses significant regulatory non-compliance risks under data protection laws.

LocalDoc parses the OpenXML structure of the DOCX container, reads the underlying document.xml nodes, and renders them into vector PDF objects in local browser RAM.

2. Handling Fonts, Headings & Line Wraps in Memory

Our parser calculates character widths against standard typography metrics to execute precise word-wrapping on standardized A4 page boundaries (595.28 x 841.89 pt). Paragraph spacing, page breaks, and heading hierarchies are cleanly maintained.
