1. The Architecture of the Casio fx-5800P Super-FX Plus

Introduced by Casio as a premier natural textbook programmable calculator, the fx-5800P bridges the gap between traditional non-programmable scientific calculators (like the fx-991EX) and bulky graphing calculators. Its signature feature is a high-resolution 4-line dot-matrix LCD display paired with Casio BASIC programming language capabilities.

📟 The 4-Line Dot-Matrix Display Explained

  • Line 1 (Status Flags & Header): Displays active modes (PRGM, D, R, G, S for Shift, A for Alpha, M for Memory, and DEC/HEX in Base-N).
  • Line 2 (Expression & Input Prompt): Displays natural mathematical expressions or user input prompts (e.g. A? 15.5) with a blinking block cursor.
  • Line 3 (Intermediate Step & Pause Indicator): Displays sub-calculations or pauses output with the blinking ◢ symbol until you press EXE.
  • Line 4 (Final Result & Ans): Shows right-aligned numerical output or Disp indicating program execution completion.
