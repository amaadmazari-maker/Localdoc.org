1. The Privacy Peril of Uploading Financial Records

Law firms, accounting practices, and healthcare providers frequently need to merge multi-page patient records, tax filings, and legal exhibits. Uploading these documents to public cloud conversion servers violates GDPR, HIPAA, and standard client confidentiality agreements.

LocalDoc merges binary PDF page trees directly inside browser memory, ensuring confidential client records never leave the physical office computer.

2. Zero-Copy Page Tree Concatenation

Our merging engine reads the indirect object dictionaries of each source PDF, remaps object reference IDs to eliminate collisions, and copies vector page streams into a unified master catalog in milliseconds.
