1. US State Department vs. ICAO Schengen Standards

Submitting an uncompliant passport photo is the number one cause of consular visa delays. The US State Department requires a strict 2x2 inch (600x600 px @ 300 DPI) square with 50-69% head height. In contrast, European Schengen, UK, and Australian passports follow the ICAO 35x45mm standard (70-80% face coverage).
