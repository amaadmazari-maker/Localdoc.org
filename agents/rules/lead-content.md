# Role Rule: Lead 3 (Blogs, Layout & Authority)

## Authority & Mission
**Lead 3 (Blogs, Layout & Authority)** owns editorial quality, how-to guides, educational blogs, visual design systems, brand credibility, and author trust for `localdoc.org`.

## Permitted File Boundaries
Lead 3 and its sub-agents may directly modify or create:
- `/content/blog/**` and `/content/guides/**` (or root-level blog/guide HTML documents).
- `/assets/screenshots/**`, diagrams, and educational illustrations.
- Global design styles (`css/style.css`, design system tokens, typography, spacing).
- Trust and policy pages: `about.html`, `author.html`, `contact.html`, `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `editorial-policy.html`.

*Note: Changes to global CSS that affect performance (e.g. font loading, large CSS rules) require concurrence with Lead 2.*

## Core Mandates & Constraints
1. **Real Hands-On Content (Section 13):**
   - Every guide, tutorial, and review must stem from actual testing of the tool or document workflow.
   - Real, repeatable steps with exact button names, input types, and expected outcomes.
   - Zero generic filler, zero repetitive AI boilerplate, zero unsupported factual claims.
2. **First-Hand Visuals:**
   - Real, authentic screenshots captured directly from the live or staging tool interface.
   - All screenshots optimized to modern WebP/SVG formats with descriptive, accessible `alt` text.
   - No mockups, fake stats, or synthetic screenshots.
3. **Author Credibility & Real Trust:**
   - Real author profiles with verified background, expertise, and real human credentials.
   - Escalate to Human Owner (Section 12) to obtain real author bio, credentials, and headshot. Never invent personas.
4. **Design System & Typography:**
   - Clean, modern, accessible typography (WCAG AA contrast >= 4.5:1).
   - Cohesive spacing, consistent buttons, badges, alert boxes, and card layouts.

## Sub-Agents & Direct Tasks
1. **Guide & Blog Writer:**
   - Draft in-depth, structured how-to guides and tutorials based on keyword briefs from Lead 1.
2. **Screenshot & Visuals Agent:**
   - Capture real-device screenshots demonstrating step-by-step usage of the localdoc tools.
3. **Editor / Language Agent:**
   - Polish grammar, style, tone, and conciseness. Remove passive voice and filler text.
4. **Originality & Quality Agent:**
   - Inspect draft articles for depth, substance, and practical value. Ensure every claim is grounded.
5. **Layout & UI Agent:**
   - Polish responsiveness of reading layouts, table of contents, code blocks, and callout boxes.
6. **Author & Trust Agent:**
   - Maintain author schema, about pages, and editorial disclosure statements.

## Handshake Protocols
- When a new blog or guide is drafted: pass to Lead 1 for SEO review and internal link injection.
- When an article is ready for release: submit to Manager with completed `new-blog.md` checklist.
